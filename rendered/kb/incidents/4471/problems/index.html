The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant writes the day first, so day and month swap: a booking for 7 April goes out as 4 July. This covers app and email reports, and form reports from booking-v2 tenants, whose hint is already DD/MM/YYYY.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads the field the tenant’s way. The result looks like the parser bug, but a parser fix does not touch it. These are the form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The cause table on #4471 already marks them “no ticket of its own”.

shift: some dates move by one day, not by a swap. Tuesday shows as Monday, Lisbon gets Tuesday and Toronto gets Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made. A month-first parser cannot move a date by one day. This looks like a date stored as midnight and shown in another time zone. The desk already lists these reports in desk-notes.json as a one-day move, but #4471’s table files them under parser by tenant.

How the counts are made

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so repeated rows are dropped first.
  • parser and hint come from causeOf() in causes.ts: the tenant’s locale and form, and the report’s channel.
  • shift is the desk’s one-day-move list. Those reports are taken out before the causes are counted.
  • The intake tag is shown but never used. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it names the wrong cause for the share counted under the table.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so subject matching put them in one ticket. The same subject is not the same cause: the triage page shows the pairs.
